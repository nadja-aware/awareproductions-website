Services

01

Experience Design

Concept, narrative and guest journey for an event or experience. Two ways to start: AWARE joins your in-house creative team, or takes your brief and develops the concept from there.

02

Event & Project Management

Planning, budget, timeline and suppliers, plus management of the teams across all departments. One lead overseeing the whole project end to end.

03

On-site Production

On-site production makes sure everything runs smoothly and everyone is taken care of. Crew and suppliers are managed and on time, the run of show holds, guest flow works and the client is happy. Above all, guests experience the concept as it was planned.

04

Consultancy

For teams asking how to realise an idea, or how to improve the way things already run. AWARE reviews concepts and processes, optimises them and brings a fresh perspective to event teams.

05

Artist Management

1  Artist management and booking during pre-production, and artist care during production.

2  Booking immersive artists from the curated AWARE Productions roster.

About

An independent studio with an international network.

I'm Nadja, founder of AWARE Productions. Since 2017 I have designed and produced experiences for brands, festivals, communities and private events, for 10 to 10,000 guests. I am mostly hired by brand communication and event agencies across Europe, or directly by brands. I lead with a human-centred approach I call “freedom in a frame”, and I also manage and book artists. I work in German, English and Spanish.

One thing is non-negotiable: every production I take on raises awareness in some form. Of self, of others or of the world.

The focus. Awareness-driven experiences in wellness, holistic wellbeing, fitness, healthy living and mental health, for brands of every size, alternative communities and changemakers who want to create real impact.

The mission. To make people more aware of themselves, of each other and of the world around them, by designing for presence and connection.

The why. Over the last decade, almost everything has moved online. The zeitgeist is now shifting back to experiences in real life. AWARE draws on lived experience and on what festivals, retreats and communities have long understood, and brings it into the commercial and corporate world.

The how. Bridging visions into impact with a creative, solution-oriented mindset: there are no problems, only situations that need to be taken care of. Exceptional people from around the world are brought in to add awe, wonder and a spark of magic.

For brands. Immersive events that guests experience with all their senses. People step in, take part and connect with each other and with what the brand stands for.

What I bring: a background in project and event management and brand communication, more than 40 end-to-end events, and over 20 cross-platform projects, campaigns and product releases with budgets of up to €2 million. I always lead teams with a human-centred approach and keep a 360° overview of every department, person and task. “Freedom in a frame” is a professional structure that lets creativity flow easily within it.

Alongside production, I work closely with artists: helping creatives find orientation and structure, taking on administration and management, and securing bookings that match their goals.
